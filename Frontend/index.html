Privacy & AI Use

ResumeMaster sends the resume and job information you provide to Google Gemini through your API key to generate results. Do not upload passwords, financial information, government ID numbers, confidential business information, or other sensitive information you do not want processed by an AI service.

ResumeMaster does not intentionally store your Gemini API key. The key is held in page memory while you use the site and is cleared when you refresh or close the page. You are responsible for the information you submit and for your Gemini API key and its usage.
